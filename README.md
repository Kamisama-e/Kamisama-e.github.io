### About Me

I am an undergraduate student in the Experimental (Honors) Class of Computer Science and Technology at **Beijing University of Technology** (2024–present), ranking **1 / 68** with a weighted average score of **95.64 / 100**. I conduct research with Prof. Jinduo Liu and serve as the Principal Investigator of a Beijing Natural Science Foundation Undergraduate Research Grant (*"QiYan" Program*, CNY 50,000).

My research focuses on **causal discovery and interventional analysis**, and their principled application to **multimodal large language model (MLLM) safety**:
- **Causal Structure Learning:** Auditing cross-representation evidence persistence for exact-lag time-series causal discovery and stabilizing finite-sample RL causal graph search.
- **Causal Multimodal LLM Safety:** Isolating and nullifying post-fusion harmful image–text interaction subspaces via counterfactual contrasts and learning calibrated image-conditional refusal under fixed textual prompts.
